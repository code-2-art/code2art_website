Check ComfyUI's first-ever official conference: ComfyCon!  Our global core team gathers for the first time, in Shanghai! We would like to invite you to join this amazing gig, bringing together China's top open-source developers, creators, outstanding communities, and industry partners to explore the future of AI open-source and visual generation.
