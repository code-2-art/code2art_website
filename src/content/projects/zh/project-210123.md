5文摘

#### The Tao of Programming 编程之道 1.3节

<img src="/uploads/project-210123/img_013.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" />

In the beginning was the Tao.

The Tao gave birth to Space and Time.

宇宙之初有道。

道产生了空间和时间。

Therefore Space and Time are the Yin and Yang of programming.

空间和时间便是编程设计之阴阳。

Programmers that do not comprehend the Tao are always running out of time and space for their programs. Programmers that comprehend the Tao always have enough time and space to accomplish their goals.

不能领悟此道的编程者总是耗尽他们所要编写的程序的时间和空间。

而领悟了此道的编程者却总有足够的时间和空间来达到他们的目标。

How could it be otherwise?

除此之外，难道还有其他的情形吗？

*中文译文自《编程之道》电子工业出版社2006版。*
